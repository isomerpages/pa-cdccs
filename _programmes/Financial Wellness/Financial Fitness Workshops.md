<h1>Building financial confidence, one simple step at a time.</h1>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/IMG__113_.jpg">
</div>
<p>No matter where you are in life — from first-jobbers to young families,
mid-career switchers or retirees — our free <strong>Financial Fitness Workshops</strong> are
designed for you!</p>
<p>Whether you’re starting out or planning your next chapter, these workshops
are meant to make financial literacy accessible, relevant and doable. We
want every resident to have the knowledge and confidence to make sound
financial decisions — because financial wellbeing is a key part of living
a Better Life.</p>
<p>Each workshop is built around four pillars of financial fitness: <strong>Save, Insure, Invest and Legacy Planning</strong>&nbsp;
— a complete framework to help you take charge of your finances at every
stage of life.</p>
<p><strong>What Can You Expect?</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>A free and quick “health scan” of your <strong>Financial Fitness</strong>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Insights into <strong>Financial Guidelines</strong> within Singapore’s context
today</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Guidance on <strong>saving, insuring, investing and legacy planning</strong> based
on your life stage</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Resources to support strengthening your <strong>“Financial Muscles”</strong> and
build lasting habits</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Ready to take the first step towards a stronger financial future?</p>
